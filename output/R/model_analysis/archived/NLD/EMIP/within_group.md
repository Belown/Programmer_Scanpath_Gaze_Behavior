# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.286: About 29% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈71% is residual.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.246: Fixed effects explain some additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 24.58% of the variance; fixed effects explain ~14.05% (total 38.63%).
3. **Post-hoc inference**:
   - **Expertise**: Mostly non-significant; only *none vs medium* is significant (p = 0.0308) with a small difference; *none vs low* is marginal (p = 0.0522).
   - **Stimulus**: Strong effect; rectangle > vehicle (estimate = 0.0343, p < 0.0001).

## Summary

- **Stimulus has a clear effect** on NLD score.
- **Expertise effects are weak and inconsistent**, with only one small pairwise difference reaching significance.
