# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.319: Approximately 32% of total variance is attributable to stable differences among individuals across pairings, while ≈68% reflects pairing-specific residual variability.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.318: Fixed effects explain virtually no additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 31.88% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~0.05%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No expertise effects. Both expertise_mean (p = 0.588) and expertise_diff (p = 0.708) are non-significant with near-zero slopes.

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict NLD score.**
- **Individual differences explain a modest share** of variance (~32%), with most variability remaining residual.
