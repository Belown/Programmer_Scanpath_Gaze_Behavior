# Summary

## 1) Individual Differences Dominate

- **Shape/Length** show high ICCs (~0.65–0.83): strong, persistent individual visual strategies.
- **Random effects explain most variance** (≈65–83%) across models and settings.

## 2) Expertise Effects are Absent

- **Mean expertise, expertise difference, and pair type are non-significant** in all dimensions.
- **Fixed effects explain <1–2%** of variance.

## 3) Dimension-Specific Structure

- **Direction/Position** have low ICCs (≈0.25–0.44): large residual variance (~60–70%).
- These dimensions are more **context- or interaction-dependent**.

## 4) Stimulus Effects (Within-Group)

- Expertise still non-significant.
- Significant but small stimulus differences were observed for Shape, Position, and Duration (Length marginal; Direction non-significant).

## Interpretation

- **Who the participant is matters more than expertise level.**
- **Expertise does not systematically shape scanpath similarity.**
- **Stimulus context can modulate similarity**, but effects are modest.
