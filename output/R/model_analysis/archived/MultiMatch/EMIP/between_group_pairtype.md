## Summary

- **No expertise pairing effects**: Across all scanpath dimensions, expertise pair composition (Expert–Expert, Novice–Novice, Expert–Novice) shows no significant impact on within-group scanpath similarity  

- **Individual variability dominates**: High ICC values for Shape (69%), Length (65%), and Duration (53%) indicate that stable individual characteristics account for the majority of similarity patterns  

- **Spatial dimensions more context-dependent**: Direction (33%) and Position (29%) exhibit lower ICC values, suggesting greater situational variability compared to temporal and shape-related dimensions  

- **Convergent evidence across analyses**: Results align with mean difference analyses, providing consistent evidence that expertise composition does not meaningfully predict gaze pattern
