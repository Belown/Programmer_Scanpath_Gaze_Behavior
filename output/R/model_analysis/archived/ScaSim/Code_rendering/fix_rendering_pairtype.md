# Model Analysis

1. **Random Effects**: Random intercept SDs for exp_a = 0.01521 and exp_b = 0.01989; residual SD = 0.04909. Adjusted ICC = 0.206, meaning that ~21% of the total variance in score is attributable to stable between-participant differences (moderate individual differences).
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Marginal R² = 0.0239; Conditional R² = 0.2255. PairType explains ~2.4% of the variance; participant-level random effects account for an additional ~20%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**:
   - BB ≈ BI (Δ ≈ +0.0115, p = .1806)
   - BB > II (Δ ≈ +0.0249, p = .0242)
   - BI ≈ II (Δ ≈ +0.0134, p = .1882)
Conclusion: BB pairs yield significantly higher score than II pairs. No reliable differences are observed between BB and BI or between BI and II. Pair composition has a small but statistically detectable effect on score, although most systematic variability is still driven by between-participant differences captured by the random effects.
