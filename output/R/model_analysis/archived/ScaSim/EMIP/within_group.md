# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.566: About 57% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈43% is residual.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.553: Fixed effects explain a small additional share.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 55.25% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~2.36%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**:
   - **Expertise**: No significant differences (all p ≥ 0.252).
   - **Stimulus**: Small but significant effect; rectangle > vehicle (estimate = 0.00451, p = 0.0358).

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict ScaSim score** within-group.
- **Stimulus has a small, significant effect**.
