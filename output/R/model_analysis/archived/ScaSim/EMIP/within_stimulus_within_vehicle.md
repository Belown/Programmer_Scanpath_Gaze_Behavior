# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.625: About 63% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈37% is residual.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.616: Fixed effects explain very little additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 61.63% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~1.35%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No expertise effects. All pairwise comparisons are non-significant (all p ≥ 0.2167), with small mean differences.

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict ScaSim score** within vehicle stimulus (within-group).
- **Individual differences account for most variance**.
