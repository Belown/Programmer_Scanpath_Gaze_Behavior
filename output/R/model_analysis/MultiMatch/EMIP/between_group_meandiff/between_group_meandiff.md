   - Adjusted ICC = 0.709: Approximately 71% of total variance is attributable to stable differences among individuals across pairing, rather than to pairing-specific residual noise.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.703: Fixed effects explain very little additional variance.

## Summary

- **Similarity driven by individual traits**: Scanpath similarity is primarily explained by stable individual characteristics rather than expertise levels  

- **Greater variability in spatial dimensions**: Direction and Position exhibit more situational variability compared to temporal and shape-related aspects  

- **No expertise composition effects**: Neither expertise homogeneity nor heterogeneity influences gaze pattern alignment in this dataset
