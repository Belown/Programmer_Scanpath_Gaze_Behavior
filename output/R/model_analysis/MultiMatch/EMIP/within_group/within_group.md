# Dimensions

## Shape

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.694: Approximately 69% of total variance is attributable to stable differences among individuals across pairings, rather than to pairing-specific residual noise.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.682: Fixed effects explain very little additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 68.23% of the variance, while fixed effects explain only a very small proportion (~1.75%).
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No expertise effect detected (all p > 0.31). Stimulus has a statistically significant but very small effect (p = 0.0021, estimate = -0.000219).

---

## Summary

- **No expertise effects**: Across all dimensions, expertise level shows no significant impact on within-group scanpath similarity
- **Stimulus effects**: Significant stimulus differences detected in Shape (p=0.002), Position (p<0.0001), and Duration (p<0.0001); marginally non-significant for Length (p=0.058); no effect for Direction
- **Individual variability dominates**: High ICC values for Shape (69%), Length (65%), and Duration (53%) indicate stable individual characteristics drive similarity
- **Spatial dimensions more variable**: Direction (33%) and Position (29%) show lower ICC, suggesting more context-dependent variability
