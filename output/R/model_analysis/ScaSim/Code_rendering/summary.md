# Summary

## 1 Rendering is the dominant determinant of score

Models including render_a (with or without expertise) show large, robust effects.

- r1 consistently outperforms r2 and r3.
- In the full rendering model: r1 > r2 > r3 (all pairwise differences significant).
- render_b shows only weak or inconsistent effects.
- Rendering explains a large share of variance (Marginal R² ≈ 0.34–0.36).
- Between-participant variability is also substantial (ICC ≈ 0.40).

## 2 Expertise modulates rendering weakly and inconsistently

When expertise_a × render_a is included:

- Beginners score slightly higher than Intermediates overall (Δ ≈ +0.024, p = .026).
- The dominant effect remains render_a (r1 > r2, r1 > r3; r2 ≈ r3).
- Fixed effects explain ~36% of variance, but most of that comes from rendering, not expertise.

## 3 PairType has a small but detectable effect (BB > II)

When rendering is not included:

- BB pairs score significantly higher than II pairs (Δ ≈ +0.025, p = .024).
- BB ≈ BI and BI ≈ II.
- PairType explains ~2.4% of variance; most structure comes from random effects.

## 4 Continuous expertise metrics show a small negative effect

Using expertise_mean and expertise_diff:

- expertise_mean has a small but statistically reliable negative association with score (β ≈ −0.025, p = .0089).
- expertise_diff has no effect.
- Fixed effects explain ~2.4% of variance; participant random effects dominate.

## Overall conclusions

- Rendering (render_a) is by far the strongest and most reliable predictor of score.
- Expertise effects are weak, inconsistent, or marginal; they slightly modulate performance but do not drive it.
- Pair composition has a small effect (BB > II) when rendering is not controlled.
- Mean expertise shows a small negative association with score; expertise asymmetry does not matter.
- Between-participant variability is consistently large (ICC ≈ 0.20–0.40), exceeding most fixed-effect contributions.
Performance differences are driven overwhelmingly by rendering quality,t with only minor and inconsistent contribuions from expertise level or pair composition.
