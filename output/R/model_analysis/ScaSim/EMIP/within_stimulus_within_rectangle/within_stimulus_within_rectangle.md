   - Adjusted ICC = 0.675: About 68% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈32% is residual.

2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 65.26% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~3.50%.

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict ScaSim score** within rectangle stimulus (within-group).
- **Individual differences account for most variance**.
