# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.639: About 64% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈36% is residual.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.634: Fixed effects explain very little additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 63.40% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~0.76%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No expertise effects. All pairwise comparisons are non-significant (all p ≥ 0.1432), with small mean differences.

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict ScaSim score** within rectangle stimulus.
- **Individual differences account for most variance**.
