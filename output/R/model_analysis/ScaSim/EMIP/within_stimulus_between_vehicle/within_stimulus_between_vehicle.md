# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.599: About 60% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈40% is residual.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.598: Fixed effects explain virtually no additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 59.75% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~0.28%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No expertise effects. All pairwise comparisons are non-significant (all p ≥ 0.5997), with very small mean differences.

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict ScaSim score** within vehicle stimulus.
- **Individual differences account for most variance**.
