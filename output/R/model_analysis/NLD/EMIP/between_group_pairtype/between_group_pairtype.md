1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.315: Approximately 32% of total variance is attributable to stable differences among individuals across pairings, while ≈68% reflects pairing-specific residual variability.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.313: Fixed effects explain virtually no additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 31.89% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~0.59%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No PairType effects. All pairwise comparisons are non-significant (all p ≥ 0.56), with very small mean differences across categories (emmeans ~0.879–0.891).

## Summary

- **PairType does not predict NLD score.**
- **Individual differences explain a modest share** of variance (~32%), with most variability remaining residual.
