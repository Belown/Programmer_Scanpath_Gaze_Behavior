# Score

1. **Random Effects**:
   - Adjusted ICC = 0.315: About 32% of variance reflects stable individual differences across pairings, while ≈68% is residual.
   - Unadjusted ICC = 0.315: Fixed effects explain virtually no additional variance.
2. **Nakagawa R²**: Random effects account for 31.53% of the variance, while fixed effects explain ~0.07%.
3. **Post-hoc inference**: No expertise effects. All pairwise comparisons are non-significant (all p ≥ 0.74) with very small mean differences.

## Summary

- **Expertise does not predict NLD score** within rectangle stimulus.
- **Most variance remains residual**, with modest individual-level structure.
